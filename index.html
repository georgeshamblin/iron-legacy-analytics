Finding 2

Why it stopped: the website sends data somewhere else

Every GA4 property has a Measurement ID. The website has to carry that exact ID or the property sees nothing. Ironlegacyadvisors.com carries two IDs today, and neither one belongs to the property we were given.

Property we were givenG-M461CZR882

Not found anywhere on the site.

On the site nowG-HSJM6H6B4J

Probably the site's main analytics. Owner unknown.

On the site nowG-90NR43CZBZ

FMG Suite's own platform tag.

The website runs on FMG Suite. FMG adds its own tracking to every advisor site. The likeliest story is that the site was rebuilt or switched tags in fall 2023, and this property was left behind. The data from the last two years probably exists, but it's in G-HSJM6H6B4J, and nobody on our side has access to it yet.

The data stream is also set to http://ironlegacyadvisors.com. The live site is https://www.ironlegacyadvisors.com. That doesn't stop tracking, but it should match.

Suggestions

Laying the foundation

Do these in order. Steps 1 and 2 bring the numbers back. Steps 3 to 7 make sure the numbers are worth reading. Tick each one off as it's done. Your progress is saved in this browser.

  1. Ask FMG support which Google account holds that tag. If it's Brian's, add clients@rankprompt.com as an Administrator. Then you get the last two years of data back too.

  2. Either keep G-HSJM6H6B4J or put G-M461CZR882 in FMG's custom tracking field. One property, one ID. Then fix the stream URL to https://www.

  3. Count contact form sends, phone taps, email clicks and "schedule a meeting" clicks. The 2023 data already shows tel and mailto clicks happen. Today they just aren't counted as leads.

  4. Exclude the office IP so staff visits don't count, and watch the "Unassigned" channel. In 2023, a third of the visits were junk.

  5. Verify the domain and link it to GA4. That shows which searches bring people in, which matters most because search visitors engage best.

  6. GA4 defaults to 2 months for detailed reports. Fourteen months lets us compare this year with last year.

  7. After 30 clean days we have a real baseline: visits, search visits and leads. Every report after that measures against it.

Before we start
